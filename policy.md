## 2. The short version

- Feeziq requires an account. You cannot use the app anonymously.
- We collect the health and fitness information you enter — weight, workouts, meals, hydration, goals, body metrics — because that is the product. Under EU/UK law this is **special-category health data**, and we process it only with your **explicit consent**, which you give when you create an account and can withdraw at any time.
- **We do not sell your personal data**, and we do not share your health data with advertisers.
- Parts of your profile are **public to other Feeziq users** by design: username, display name, photo, biography, sports, XP, streaks, achievements and leaderboard standing.
- AI features send your input (a meal photo, a text description, your fitness profile) to **Google's Vertex AI**. Google processes it as our service provider and, under our Google Cloud terms, **does not use it to train its models**.
- Free accounts see **Google AdMob** ads. Ad SDKs collect device and advertising identifiers. They do not receive your health data.
- You can delete your account and its data at any time at **feeziq.com**.

## 3. What we collect

### 3.1 Account and identity

| Data | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | You, or your Google / Apple sign-in | Authentication, account recovery, service notices |
| Password (hashed) | You | Authentication. We never see your password in readable form — Firebase Authentication handles it |
| Google / Apple account identifier | Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple | Authentication. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever hold the relay address |
| User ID | Generated by us | Ties your records together |
| Display name, username, profile photo, biography | You | Your public profile |
| Referral code / who invited you | You | Referral rewards |

### 3.2 Health and fitness data (special category)

- Date of birth (and the age derived from it), gender, height
- Weight entries and weight history
- Physical activity level, fitness goal, sports you practise
- Dietary triggers, allergies and restrictions you record
- Workouts created, scheduled and completed, including per-set and per-exercise detail
- Diet plans, meals logged, portion sizes and nutrition totals
- Hydration entries
- Free-text activity descriptions you write for AI recognition
- Streaks, XP, progress records and achievements

We process this **only on the basis of your explicit consent** (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a)). Withdrawing consent means deleting your account, because the app cannot function without this data.

### 3.3 Content you create

- **Direct messages** you send to other users. Message content is encrypted with AES before it is written to our database and to your device. This is encryption at rest, **not end-to-end encryption**: the key is managed by the app, so we retain the technical ability to decrypt messages, which we do only where required by law or to investigate abuse reported to us.
- **Photos** you upload: profile picture, workout banners, diet banners. These are stored in Firebase Storage.
- **Meal photos** you scan with the AI meal scanner. These are read from your device and sent to Google's Vertex AI for analysis. **They are not uploaded to our storage and we do not keep them** — only the nutrition estimates you choose to save.
- **AI Coach conversations**, including the profile summary we attach so the coach can answer usefully.
- Ratings, reports and block lists.

### 3.4 Device, session and technical data

Collected automatically each time you sign in or use the app:

- Device model, operating system and version, and whether the device is physical or an emulator
- A device identifier used to distinguish your sessions
- **IP address**, and the **approximate country and city derived from it** via the third-party lookup service `freegeoip.app`. Feeziq does **not** request or use GPS or precise device location.
- Login and last-active timestamps
- Firebase Cloud Messaging push tokens
- App version, language and locale
- Crash and diagnostic information

You can view every active session and sign a device out remotely from **Settings → Sessions**.

### 3.5 Usage and analytics data

Through Google Analytics for Firebase: screens viewed, features used, AI features invoked, workouts started and completed, subscription and purchase events, and your user ID so events can be attributed to your account. Sign out and we stop attributing events to you.

### 3.6 Advertising data

If you are on a free plan, Google AdMob serves banner and rewarded ads and may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data. On iOS, Feeziq **does not present the App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not request permission to track you across apps**, so no cross-app tracking identifier is made available to advertisers through us. Ads are removed entirely on a paid subscription.

### 3.7 Purchase data

Subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play and managed by **RevenueCat**. RevenueCat receives your Feeziq user ID, purchase receipts, entitlement status, product and transaction identifiers, and device platform. **We never receive or store your card number, bank details or billing address** — those stay with Apple and Google.

### 3.8 What we do not collect

- Precise or background location
- Contacts, calendar, SMS or call logs
- HealthKit or Google Fit data
- Audio recordings. The microphone permission string that ships in the iOS app exists only because the system media picker declares it; Feeziq records no audio.
- Biometric identifiers, government IDs, or payment card data

## 5. AI features in detail

Feeziq uses **Google Vertex AI (Gemini models)** through Firebase AI Logic for:

- **Meal photo scanning** — the photo you take or choose is sent to Gemini for nutritional estimation.
- **Meal and activity description** — the text you type is sent to Gemini.
- **Workout and diet plan generation** — your fitness profile (age, gender, height, weight, activity level, goal, sports, dietary restrictions) is sent to Gemini.
- **AI Coach** — your messages plus a summary of your fitness profile are sent to Gemini for each conversation.

What you should know:

1. Google acts as our **processor** for these calls. Under the Google Cloud / Vertex AI terms that apply to Feeziq, **your prompts and responses are not used to train Google's models**.
2. AI output is an **estimate**. Calorie counts, macros and plans are approximations and are frequently wrong in detail.
3. **Do not type medical details, diagnoses, medication or anything else you would not want processed by a third-party AI service** into free-text AI fields. Only send what the feature needs.
4. AI conversations and generated plans you save are stored with your account and are deleted when you delete your account.

## 7. What other users can see

Feeziq is social. By design, other users can see:

- Your username, display name, profile photo and biography
- Your sports, XP, streak, achievements, follower and following counts
- Your position on public and monthly leaderboards
- Your presence indicator inside a chat you share with them
- Your messages, to the person you send them to
- Aggregated, non-identifying contributions to the community activity feed and global statistics

Other users **cannot** see your email address, date of birth, weight, meals, hydration, body metrics, sessions or device information.

You can block a user at any time, which stops messages in both directions.

## 8. Retention

- **Account and health data:** kept while your account exists.
- **After account deletion:** removed from live systems promptly, and purged from encrypted backups within **90 days**.
- **Messages:** deleted with the account. A message you sent remains visible in the recipient's copy of the conversation until they delete it.
- **Session records:** retained while the session is active; revoked sessions are deleted.
- **Analytics:** retained by Google Analytics for Firebase for a maximum of **14 months**.
- **Transaction and tax records:** retained as long as tax and accounting law requires (typically 7 years), independently of account deletion.

## 9. Security

- All traffic between the app and our backend uses TLS.
- Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never hold your plaintext password.
- Direct-message content is AES-encrypted before it is written to the database and to local storage; the encryption key is held in the platform keystore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) rather than in app code.
- Requests are attested with **Firebase App Check** to reduce abuse of our backend from unauthorised clients.
- Access to production data is limited to the operator of Feeziq.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data and creates a risk to you, we will notify you and the relevant supervisory authority as required by law (within 72 hours of becoming aware, under GDPR).

## 11. Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy
- **Correct** anything inaccurate — most profile and health fields are editable in the app
- **Delete** your account and data
- **Export** your data in a portable, machine-readable format
- **Restrict** or **object to** processing based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, including consent to health-data processing
- Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights

**How to exercise them.** Delete your account yourself at **feeziq.com** (Settings → Account → Delete Account links you there), or email **[privacy@feeziq.com]**. We respond within **30 days**, extendable by a further 60 days for complex requests, and we will tell you if we need more time. We may ask you to verify control of your account email before acting on a request.

### If you are in California

Under the CCPA/CPRA you have the rights above, plus the right to know the categories of personal information collected, the sources, the business purpose, and the categories of third parties involved — all set out in Sections 3 and 6.

**We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.** Feeziq does not request App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS. The health and fitness information you enter is **sensitive personal information** under the CPRA; we use it only to provide the service you asked for and for no secondary purpose that would trigger a right to limit its use.

### If you are in the EEA or UK

You additionally have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
